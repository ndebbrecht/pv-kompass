# Icon-Erzeugung

Mit dem integrierten Bildgenerierungswerkzeug erstellt. Originalmaster: 1254 × 1254 Pixel. Für iOS mit `sips -z 1024 1024` auf 1024 × 1024 Pixel normiert; PNG ohne Alphakanal. Das Motiv wurde dabei nicht verändert. Die frühere AppIcon.png bleibt in der Git-Historie erhalten.

## Verwendeter Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished, original iOS app icon for 'PV Kompass', an accessible utility that measures the tilt and compass orientation of photovoltaic solar panels. Asset: single square 1024 x 1024 app-icon master, fully opaque, full-bleed square, NO baked-in rounded corners and NO surrounding margin or device frame. A bold simple geometric blue photovoltaic panel viewed as a slightly tilted trapezoid with just a few crisp pale grid lines; above it a warm golden solar arc and one clear elegant compass needle pointing diagonally upward. Integrate these into one coherent confident emblem, not separate clip-art. Deep midnight-blue background with extremely subtle tonal depth, warm amber/gold accent echoing the app's ochre controls, luminous restrained light-blue panel. Premium minimal flat/vector-like precision with subtle refined depth, strong silhouette, excellent recognition at tiny home-screen size, generous internal breathing room. Centered balanced composition, no busy compass tick marks. No text, no letters, no numerals, no logos of other brands, no watermark, no photographic objects, no gloss, no drop shadow outside icon. Output ONLY the finished square icon.
